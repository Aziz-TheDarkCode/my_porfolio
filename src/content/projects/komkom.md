Designed and developed the platform's landing page using ![Drupal](/icons/drupal.svg) **Drupal**, ![Twig](/icons/twig.svg) **Twig**, ![PHP](https://svgl.app/library/php.svg) **PHP**, and ![Tailwind CSS](https://svgl.app/library/tailwindcss.svg) **Tailwind CSS**, implementing a waitlist registration system and a referral mechanism that generated more than **350 sign-ups at launch**.

Built the internal back-office application with ![Angular](https://svgl.app/library/angular.svg) **Angular** and ![TypeScript](https://svgl.app/library/typescript.svg) **TypeScript**, enabling user management, tontine tracking, withdrawal processing, and bulk notification management, integrating backend services through ![Swagger](https://svgl.app/library/swagger.svg) **Swagger**-documented APIs.
