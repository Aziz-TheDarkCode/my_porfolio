Designed a modular backend architecture with ![Fastify](https://svgl.app/library/fastify.svg) **Fastify** and ![Node.js](https://svgl.app/library/nodejs.svg) **Node.js**, implementing a ledger-based transactional system for consistency, auditability, and integrity across financial flows, with idempotent APIs for payments, orders, and partner management.

Optimized application performance through ![Redis](https://svgl.app/library/redis.svg) **Redis** caching and session management, and refactored critical queries on ![Appwrite](https://svgl.app/library/appwrite.svg) **Appwrite**.

Built the marketing frontend and internal application using ![React](https://svgl.app/library/react_light.svg) **React**, ![TypeScript](https://svgl.app/library/typescript.svg) **TypeScript**, ![Tailwind CSS](https://svgl.app/library/tailwindcss.svg) **Tailwind CSS**, and ![Zustand](/icons/zustand.svg) **Zustand**, automating testing with ![Vitest](https://svgl.app/library/vitest.svg) **Vitest** and continuous deployment through ![Docker](https://svgl.app/library/docker.svg) **Docker** and ![GitHub Actions](/icons/github-actions.svg) **GitHub Actions**.
