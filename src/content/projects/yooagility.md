Built a multilingual, SEO-optimized static website with ![Next.js](https://svgl.app/library/nextjs_icon_dark.svg) **Next.js** (static site generator) and ![TypeScript](https://svgl.app/library/typescript.svg) **TypeScript**, showcasing the firm’s activities, articles, and training programs.

Integrated ![Directus](https://svgl.app/library/directus.svg) **Directus** as a headless CMS, enabling the team to manage content autonomously, and designed a training registration system with seat availability management.
